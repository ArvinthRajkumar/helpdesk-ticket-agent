## KB-01 Reset your password
On the login page, choose "Forgot password" under the password field. Enter the account
email. A reset link arrives by email within a few minutes. If it does not arrive, check the
spam folder and confirm the email matches the account.

## KB-02 Download invoices
Account owners can download past invoices under Settings, then Billing, then Invoice
history. Each invoice is a PDF. Only the account owner role sees the Billing page.

## KB-03 Update a payment card
Account owners can change the card under Settings, then Billing, then Payment method. The
new card is used for the next charge. A failed payment is retried automatically after the
card is updated.

## KB-04 Guest access
Guests can be invited to a single project with view-only access. Guest seats are free on the
Team plan and above. Guests cannot edit tasks or see other projects.

## KB-05 Export data
Projects can be exported as CSV from the project menu, then Export. Excel (xlsx) export is
not available today. CSV files open in Excel.

## KB-06 Email notification timing
Email notifications are sent in batches and normally arrive within a few minutes. Instant
delivery is not guaranteed. In-app notifications are immediate.

## KB-07 REST API rate limits
The REST API allows 60 requests per minute per API key. Requests above that return HTTP
429. Spread bulk syncs over time or contact support about a higher limit for your plan.

## KB-08 Single sign-on
SAML single sign-on is available on the Enterprise plan. It is not available on Starter or
Team. Contact sales for Enterprise details.

## KB-09 Integrations
Native integrations exist for Slack (comments and task updates can be posted to a channel)
and Google Calendar. Set them up under Settings, then Integrations.

## KB-10 Support hours
Support answers by email on working days. Security reports should be sent to the security
contact listed on the website's security page. We do not process security details in
regular support tickets.
